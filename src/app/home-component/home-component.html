Welcome to the Gym Leaders Archive

This application documents the Gym Leaders of two founding regions of the Pokemon world. Use the navigation bar above to explore each region's Gym Leaders, their badges, and their Pokemon teams.

Kanto Region

Home to the first 151 Pokémon and Professor Oak, Kanto represents the birth of modern Pokémon training and research.Technology & Experimentation: Known for advanced corporations like Silph Co. (creator of the Master Ball) and the Pokémon Mansion on Cinnabar Island, which was the site of genetic experiments that led to the creation of Mewtwo.

Johto Region

Johto is located west of Kanto, which together form a joint landmass that is south of Sinnoh and Sinjoh Ruins. It was the second core series region to be introduced. First explored in Pokémon Gold and Silver, it is home to an additional 100 Pokémon that were not present in the previous games. It is also the setting of Pokémon Crystal, Pokémon HeartGold, and Pokémon SoulSilver. Players begin their journey in New Bark Town, where Professor Elm offers either Chikorita, Cyndaquil, or Totodile to beginning Pokémon Trainers.
